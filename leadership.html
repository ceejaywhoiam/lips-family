// Our Story

How We Got Here

2019
LIPS FAMILY Founded

Cornelius and Remy start painting walls in Opelousas with a small crew of 8. The name LIPS FAMILY is born.

2020
First Community Event

During lockdown, LIPS FAMILY organizes outdoor art installations and socially distanced cyphers to keep the culture alive.

2021
Merch Line Launch

First limited-edition drop sells out in 72 hours. The brand goes regional overnight.

2022
Expanded to 5 Cities

LIPS FAMILY chapters open in Lafayette, Baton Rouge, New Orleans, Shreveport, and Houston.

2024
Youth Programs Launch

500+ youth served through workshops, mentorship, and free creative arts programming across Louisiana.

2026
500+ Members — 12 Cities

LIPS FAMILY reaches its largest membership yet and launches this digital home. The next chapter begins.
