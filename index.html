// Who We Are

We Are
LIPS FAMILY

LIPS FAMILY started as a graffiti collective and evolved into a full movement — music, art, community, and culture united under one name. We don't just create; we build platforms for the next generation.

From walls to stages, from studios to streets — we are everywhere art lives and breathes. Every member is family, every city is home.
