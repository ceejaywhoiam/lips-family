// Quick Answers

FAQ

How do I join LIPS FAMILY?
Head to our Recruiting page and fill out the application form. We review every submission and reach back out within 7–10 business days if it's a fit.
Can I book LIPS FAMILY for my event?
Absolutely. Use the contact form above and select "Booking Request" as the inquiry type. Share your event date, location, expected attendance, and what you're looking for (DJ, live art, performance, etc.).
Do you ship merch nationally?
Yes! We ship anywhere in the US. Processing takes 2–3 business days, then 5–7 days for standard shipping. Expedited options available — just mention it in your order message.
How can my business partner with LIPS FAMILY?
We're always open to aligned partnerships — brands, venues, local businesses, nonprofits. Use the contact form and select "Partnership / Sponsorship." We'll set up a call to explore options.
Is there a membership fee?
Core membership is free. There are optional paid tiers that unlock exclusive merch discounts, early event access, and studio time. Details shared during your onboarding conversation.
How do I sign up for youth programs?
Use the contact form and select "Youth Program Info." Parents/guardians should provide the youth's name, age, city, and any specific creative interests. Spots fill fast — register early.
